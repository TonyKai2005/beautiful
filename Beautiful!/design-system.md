# e Gain cinematic design system

## Source of truth

- Accepted concept: `/Users/zhangyinghe/.codex/generated_images/019f7100-14cc-7ae2-80e7-a0661f0386a1/exec-f859a180-49c6-4c36-b3ae-f5a9f4fb7d8b.png`
- Primary motif: a physical orange signal rail passing through Build, Test and Deploy machinery before crossing a diagonal Protect plane into a black cloud/data topology.
- Container model: one full-bleed 3D canvas with edge UI. No cards, bento grids, conventional content sections, dashboard chrome or pill labels.

## Tokens

- Warm white: `#f2efe8`
- Ink: `#070809`
- Signal orange: `#ff3d00`
- Muted warm: `#b9b6af`
- Hairline on dark: `rgba(255,255,255,.18)`
- Hairline on light: `rgba(7,8,9,.18)`
- Display: Barlow Condensed 900, uppercase, tightly tracked
- UI/body: Manrope 400/600
- Motion: long holds, short accelerations, controlled overshoot only on physical modules
- Finish: subtle PMREM metal reflections, restrained signal-only bloom, sub-pixel film finish, and a short Protect-only chromatic strike

## Responsive intent

- Desktop: corridor perspective, giant cropped type and edge chapter rail.
- Mobile: same film order and Protect inversion, dedicated vertical camera/look paths, smaller copy, reduced post effects and bottom progress rail.
- Reduced motion: discrete chapter camera frames with breathing, particle drift and grain movement frozen.
- WebGL failure: real Blender poster with semantic chapter copy, never blank or blocked.
