Local preview privacy notice.

Development environment: this notice covers the local Project Orbit prototype only. e Gain Technologies Ltd. must publish and approve its formal company Privacy Notice before production submissions are enabled.

Information entered

The prototype can collect project requirements, technical and delivery context, your name, role, organisation, work email and any optional contact details you choose to provide.

Purpose

The information is used to review your project enquiry, prepare a structured discovery brief and contact you about that enquiry. It is not used to train an AI model and the form does not accept files, passwords or secret keys.

Storage in this preview

Unsubmitted drafts remain in this browser tab through session storage and expire after 24 hours. Submitted preview records are written to the local development database. They are not transmitted to a CRM.

Production retention

The intended production rule is to review inactive, unsuccessful enquiries after 12 months and delete or anonymise identifiable information. The company must confirm this period, its lawful basis, contact details and rights process in the formal notice.

Your choice

Do not enter passwords, access tokens, health information or other unnecessary sensitive data. You may leave the form without submitting and discard the session draft.
